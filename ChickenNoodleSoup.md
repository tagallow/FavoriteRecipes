# Chicken Noodle Soup

## Ingredients

- Chicken thigh
- Chicken stock
- White onion
- Egg noodles
- Carrots
- Parsnip
- Celery
- turnip
- Parsley
- Lemon grass
- Ginger
- Dill weed
- Green onions

## Instructions

1. Chop onion, carrots, celery, parsnip, turnip
2. Heat olive oil over med/high heat.
3. Sautee onion
4. Add carrots, celery, parsnip, turnip
5. Once vegetables are warm, add chicken stock. Set heat to high
6. While warming, add parsley, ginger, and lemon grass
7. Once simmering, add chicken thighs
8. After 15/20 minutes of rolling boil, remove chicken. Shred or chop them into bite size pieces. Add back to pot
9. Add freshly chopped dill and green onions
10. Season with a lot of salt
11. Cook noodles in soup immediately before serving.
