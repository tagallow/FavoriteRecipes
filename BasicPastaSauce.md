# Basic Pasta Sauce Recipe

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 tsp sugar (to balance acidity)
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat **olive oil** in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add **chopped onion** and sauté until soft and translucent.
3. Stir in **minced garlic, dried oregano, basil, and red pepper flakes**. Cook for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. If using **ground beef**, add now and cook until brown
5. Pour in the **crushed tomatoes** and stir well.
6. Add **sugar, salt, and black pepper** to taste.
7. **Simmer** uncovered for **20-30 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.
8. Adjust seasoning if needed and serve over your favorite pasta.
