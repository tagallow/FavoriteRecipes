# Garlic Lemon Shrimp

## Ingredients

- 1 lb. large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp minced fresh parsley
- Pasta

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet, **sauté shrimp** in oil for 3 minutes
2. Add the **garlic, lemon juice, cumin** and salt
3. Cook and stir until shrimp turn pink
4. Stir in **parsley**
5. serve with pasta or rice
