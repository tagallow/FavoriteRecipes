# Aglio e Olio

## Ingredients

- 1 lb spaghetti (or linguine)
- ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 5–6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced or finely minced
- ½ tsp crushed red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
- Kosher salt, to taste
- ¼ cup fresh flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
- ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan or Pecorino Romano (optional but recommended)
- Reserved pasta water (about 1 cup)

## Instructions

1. **Cook the pasta.** Bring a large pot of water to a boil and salt it generously — it should taste like the sea. Cook the spaghetti until just *al dente* (1–2 minutes less than the package directions). **Before draining, reserve about 1 cup of pasta water.**
2. **Infuse the oil.** While the pasta cooks, add the **olive oil** and **sliced garlic** to a large skillet over **low heat**. Let them warm together slowly, stirring occasionally, for about 3–5 minutes until the garlic turns pale golden. Do not let it brown.
3. Stir in the **red pepper flakes** and cook for another 30 seconds.
4. Build the sauce. Add about ½ cup of the reserved **pasta water** to the skillet and let it simmer for 1–2 minutes. This emulsifies with the oil to create a light, silky sauce.
5. Toss the pasta. Add the drained **spaghetti directly to the skillet**. Toss vigorously over low heat, adding more pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce coats every strand and looks glossy — not oily, not dry.
6. **Finish and serve.** Remove from heat, toss in the fresh parsley, and taste for salt. Plate immediately and top with grated Parmesan if using.

***

## Tips

- **Start with cold oil** — this is the traditional and most recommended method.
- **Don't skip the pasta water** — it's the secret to a creamy, emulsified sauce rather than greasy noodles.
- **Slice, don't press** the garlic for better texture and more even cooking.
- **Serve immediately** — aglio e olio waits for no one; it tightens up quickly as it cools.
- A squeeze of fresh lemon juice at the end brightens the whole dish.
