# Chicken Alfredo with Peas

## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breasts boneless and skinless cut into cubes
- salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic minced
- 2 cups chicken broth low sodium
- 1½ cups heavy cream
- 8 ounce fettuccine
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 sprig basil
- 1½ cups Parmesan cheese grated

## Instructions

1. **Season chicken** generously with salt and pepper
2. Add **oil to a Dutch Oven** or a medium size pot and heat it over medium high heat
3. Add **chicken and brown it on all sides**, but not cooked through
4. Add **garlic** and sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant
5. Add **chicken broth, heavy cream, pasta and basil** sprig
6. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a **simmer**.
7. Cook for about **15 to 20 minutes** or **until pasta is tender**, stirring occasionally
8. When pasta is cooked to your liking **add frozen peas** and cook for 1 more minute
9. Stir in **Parmesan cheese** and season with salt and pepper if needed
