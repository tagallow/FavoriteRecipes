# Broccoli Cheddar Soup

## Ingredients

- **4 tbsp** unsalted butter
- **1 small** yellow onion, diced
- **2 cloves** garlic, minced
- **1/4 cup** all-purpose flour
- **2 cups** chicken or vegetable broth
- **2 cups** half-and-half (or whole milk)
- **4 cups** broccoli florets, chopped into bite-sized pieces
- **1 cup** carrots, matchstick or shredded
- **8 oz** sharp cheddar cheese, freshly shredded (avoid pre-shredded bag cheese for smoother melting)
- **1/2 tsp** smoked paprika or nutmeg (optional, for depth)
- Salt and fresh black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. **Sauté the aromatics:** Melt the butter in a large Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 3–4 minutes until soft and translucent. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for another 1 minute until fragrant.
2. **Make the roux:** Sprinkle the flour over the onion and garlic mixture. Whisk constantly for 1–2 minutes to cook out the raw flour taste, creating a light golden paste.
3. **Build the base:** Slowly pour in the broth and half-and-half, whisking continuously to prevent lumps. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer, whisking occasionally until it begins to thicken (about 3–5 minutes).
4. **Cook the vegetables:** Add the chopped broccoli florets, shredded carrots, paprika/nutmeg, salt, and pepper. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer gently for 10–12 minutes until the broccoli is fork-tender.
5. **Melt the cheese:** Remove the pot from the heat completely to prevent the cheese from separating or getting grainy. Stir in the freshly shredded sharp cheddar cheese one handful at a time until completely melted and smooth.
6. **Adjust texture:** For a smoother consistency, use an immersion blender to pulse a few times, leaving plenty of chunky broccoli pieces intact. Taste and adjust salt and pepper if needed. Serve hot in bowls or bread bowls with crusty bread.
