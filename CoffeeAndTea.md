# Coffee and Tea

## Coffee

### Basic Ratios

- Pour Over 60g/L
- Infusion 75g/L

### Espresso

- 13g ground coffee
- 26g espresso out

### AeroPress

- 11g coffee
- 200g water

### Hario V60 (Normal)

- 30g ground coffee
- 90ish grams water for bloom, 30 seconds
- Pour remaining water until scale reads 500g

### V60 Switch Hot

- 15g coffee / 250g water
- 2 minute steep, stir, wait 15 seconds
- release

### V60 Switch Iced

- 30g Coffee + 260g Water
- Steep 5 minutes, stir, wait 30 seconds
- Place ice in glass before releasing

### French Press

- 35g medium coarse grounded Coffee + 500 ml water
- leave it 4 minutes
- mix a bit, remove foam and fines
- let it rest 5 or more minutes
- After that, press the filter just until the surface and pour the coffee through it (without pressing the plunge all the way).

## Tea

### Matcha latte

- 1.5 teaspoons matcha powder
- 1 tablespoon hot water
- ¾ cup steamed milk

### London Fog Latte

- 1 tsp Earl Grey tea
- 120g water
- 180g steamed milk
