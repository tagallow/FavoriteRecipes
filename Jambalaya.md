# Jambalaya

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 cup chopped onion
- 2 stalks chopped celery
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 6 ounces andouille sausage (or spicy Italian sausage)
- 1 lb chicken
- 1 cup uncooked long-grain white rice
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic salt
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 cups fat-free, less-sodium chicken broth
- 3/4 cup water
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1/2 teaspoon hot pepper sauce
- 1 (14.5-ounce) can no salt-added diced tomatoes, undrained
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
- 1/2 pound peeled and deveined medium shrimp

## Instructions

1. Heat **olive oil** in a large **Dutch oven** over **medium-high** heat
2. Brown the **sausage** (about 3-4 minutes) remove and set aside.
3. Cook the **chicken** (about 5 minutes) remove and set side.
4. Add **onion**, **celery,** and **green pepper**; sauté **5 minutes** or until vegetables are tender.
    1. add **garlic** in the last 1 minute
5. Return **sausage** and **chicken** to the pot.
6. Add **rice** and the **next 7 ingredients** (rice through bay leaf); cook **2 minutes**.
7. Add **broth**, **water**, **tomato paste, hot pepper sauce**, and **diced tomatoes**; bring to a boil.
8. **Cover**, reduce heat, and **simmer 20 minutes**.
9. Add **shrimp**; cook 5 minutes.
10. Let stand 5 minutes.
11. Discard bay leaf. Stir in **parsley**.
