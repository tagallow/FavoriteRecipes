# Instant Pot Chili

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon (15ml) olive oil
- 1 pound - 1 ½ pound ground beef
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 - 2 tablespoons (8g - 16g) chili powder (depends on your chili powder’s spiciness & preference)
- 1 tablespoon (6g) cumin seed, ground
- 1 teaspoon (1g) dried oregano
- 2 cans (540ml/19oz) red kidney beans, drained & rinsed
- 1 can (28oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup (250ml) unsalted chicken stock
- 2 tablespoons (30ml) fish sauce
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) light soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons (49g) tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon (2.5g) unsweetened cocoa powder

Optional:

- 1 tablespoon (15ml) apple cider vinegar
- Brown sugar
- salt and pepper
- Green onions
- Oyster crackers
- Cheddar cheese

## Steps

1. Prepare the Pressure Cooker:
    1. Heat up your pressure cooker (Instant Pot: press Sauté button and adjust once to Sauté More function) over **medium high heat**. Ensure your pot is as hot as it can be (Instant Pot: wait until indicator says HOT).
2. **Brown the Ground Beef**:
    1. Add 1 tbsp (15ml) **olive oil** in pressure cooker. Ensure to coat the oil over whole bottom of the pot.
    2. Add ground beef in pressure cooker.
    3. The ground beef will start to release moisture.
    4. At the **5-minute** mark, **remove** the **ground beef juice** and reserve it in a small mixing bowl.
    5. Allow the ground beef to brown. Stir occasionally until they are slightly crisped and **browned** (**~5 – 7 minutes**).
3. **Mix** the **Chicken Stock Mixture** while ground beef is browning
    1. 1 cup (250ml) unsalted chicken stock,
    2. 2 tbsp (30ml) fish sauce,
    3. 1 tbsp (15ml) light soy sauce,
    4. 3 tbsp (49g) tomato paste, and
    5. 1 tsp (2.5g) unsweetened cocoa powder in a 500ml measuring cup.
4. **Sauté Onion, Garlic** and **Spices**:
    1. Add diced onions,
    2. minced garlic,
    3. 1 tbsp ground **cumin** seed,
    4. 2 tbsp **chili powder**,
    5. 1 tsp dried **oregano**.
    6. Sauté for about 5 minutes until the spices start to release their fragrance. Stir frequently.
5. Deglaze: Pour in ½ cup (125ml) of the Umami **Chicken Stock Mixture**, fully deglaze the bottom of the pot by scrubbing all the flavorful brown bits with a wooden spoon. Mix the brown bits with the stock mixture.
6. Pressure Cook the Chili:
    1. Add in the reserved **beef juice** (from Step 2).
    2. Pour in the remaining Umami **Chicken Stock Mixture**.
    3. Add 2 cans of drained **red kidney beans**. Mix well.
    4. Pour in 1 can of **crushed tomatoes** with all the juice on top. Do not mix.
    5. Close lid and pressure cook at **High Pressure for 10 minutes** + **Natural Release**. Open the lid carefully.
7. Reduce and Season: If the chili is too runny, give it a quick stir and cook for another few minutes over medium heat (Instant Pot: Press Sauté button).
    1. Taste and season with **brown sugar** (we used 1 tbsp), kosher salt and black pepper.
8. Optional: Add 1 tbsp of **Apple cider vinegar**. Note that the vinegar will significantly change the flavor profile.
9. Serve: Garnish chili with optional ingredients. Enjoy!
