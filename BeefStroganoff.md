# Beef Stroganoff

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon butter
- 8 ounces sliced fresh mushrooms
- 1 large onion, diced
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1 ¼ lbs. lean ground beef
- ¼ teaspoon dried thyme (or ¾ teaspoon fresh thyme leaves)
- ¼ teaspoon dried rosemary (or ¾ teaspoon minced fresh rosemary)
- 1 ½ cups beef broth, divided
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup sour cream
- Cooked egg noodles or rice, for serving
- Optional garnish: chopped fresh parsley

## Instructions

1. **Melt butter** in a large skillet over medium heat.
    1. **Add mushrooms, onion, and garlic** to the skillet and sauté until tender (about 8-9 minutes).
    2. Remove from skillet and set aside on a plate.
2. **Brown the ground beef** in the same skillet over medium-high heat until no longer pink (about 7 minutes), breaking it into small crumbles as it cooks. If you're not using extra lean ground beef, **drain off the excess fat**.
3. Add **thyme, rosemary, 1 cup of the broth, salt, and Worcestershire sauce**
    1. **Bring to a boil**, then reduce heat to low. **Cover and simmer for about 10 minutes**.
4. In a small bowl, **whisk together the remaining ½ cup of broth with the flour** until completely smooth.
    1. **Stir the slurry into the beef mixture.**
    2. Add the **onion and mushroom mixture back to the skillet** and bring to a boil, stirring constantly. Boil and stir for 1 minute, then reduce heat to low.
5. **Stir in the sour cream** and keep over low heat, just until warmed through (**do not boil**).
6. Taste and season with additional salt and pepper, if necessary.
7. Serve over **cooked egg noodles** or rice and garnish with fresh parsley, if desired.
